Reel

Finds and downloads music videos for songs in your music library and installs them into your Music Videos library folder as {Artist} - {Title}.mp4. Ambiguous matches are skipped — never installed.

Match confidence

A candidate video must pass every enabled gate. On any doubt it is skipped and the reason is logged with the videoId.

Candidates whose YouTube title matches any pattern (e.g. lyric, karaoke, cover, remix*) are rejected.

Discovery

Reel asks IMVDb first for official music videos, then falls back to YouTube search. Get a free key at imvdb.com/developers/apps. Video data © IMVDb (imvdb.com).

YouTube settings

If YouTube stops accepting a client version, the log will name the failing client — update the version here and restart the server. No rebuild needed.
